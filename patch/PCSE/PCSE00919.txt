# Darkest Dungeon [US]
#
# Red Hook engine (eboot.bin, module NID 0x6306BC87). The engine keeps the
# screen size in two .data words (960, 544) that get_screen_size() hands to
# the GXM device (display buffers, render target, colour + depth surfaces,
# sceDisplaySetFrameBuf) and to the canvas (the 16:9 scene targets 960x540,
# the camera and the UI scale) at start-up, so @FB is those two words.
# The scene targets are composited 1:1 into the display, so there is no
# separate internal resolution (@IB not applicable; @FB lowers everything).
# The game has no frame limiter: it paces on the display queue (one vblank
# per flip) and is GPU-bound around 30 FPS. @FPS adds the 30 FPS cap through
# the hook line; the game's own vblank wait in the display-queue callback is
# turned into two nops only when 30 is selected (60 and 20 keep the stock bytes).
# @MSAA: both sceGxmCreateRenderTarget sites build the params struct on the
# stack and store a zero register into multisampleMode; a redundant strd that
# zeroes multisampleLocations/driverMemBlock (rewritten again just before the
# call) is turned into 'mov.w ip, #msaa' and the strh takes ip. The two
# fragment-program sites and the two colour-surface scaleMode sites are
# immediates. All depth surfaces share the device's fixed depth block, which
# grows to 8 MiB at 4x; the depth stride (in samples) is doubled at 4x.
